# Plan: Extract Vision 2050 Targets for Mongolia Dashboard

## Context

Plan A Task 3 calls for adding Mongolia's Vision 2050 long-term development policy targets to compare alignment with existing NDC, NBSAP, and NAP targets. The source document is the **Vision 2050 M&E 2025 report** which monitors 87 measures across 6 goals for the Food, Agriculture, and Light Industry sector.

**Important scoping note**: This M&E report covers only the FALI (Food, Agriculture, Light Industry) ministry's subset of Vision 2050. The full Vision 2050 has 9 goals and 47 objectives — its Green Development section (Goal 6) contains many more environment/ecosystem targets that fall under the Ministry of Environment. The targets below are what's in the user-specified document, plus a few key targets from the full LTDP 2050 Green Development section that are essential for meaningful coherence analysis.

## Proposed Targets (for review)

### Source: Vision 2050 M&E report (FALI sector)

**V2050_1** — *Pasture carrying capacity*
> Establish a regionally differentiated land payment system and limit livestock numbers to match pasture carrying capacity, balancing grazing pressure across ecological zones.
- Label: "Pasture carrying capacity limits"
- Original ref: Measure 6.2.12
- Quality: Strong target — quantifiable (carrying capacity), geographically scoped (regional/zonal), directly addresses land degradation
- M&E status: 30% implementation

**V2050_2** — *Climate-resilient agriculture (goal-level)*
> Transform agriculture into an environmentally friendly, climate-change adapted, risk-resilient, socially responsive, responsible, highly productive, and sustainable leading economic sector.
- Label: "Climate-resilient agriculture"
- Original ref: Goal 8.3 (overarching goal statement)
- Quality: Broad aspirational goal — useful as framing target, but not independently measurable. Valuable for alignment analysis because it signals policy intent across the entire agriculture sector.

**V2050_3** — *Agricultural climate adaptation*
> Strengthen capacity for climate adaptation and risk reduction in agriculture, improve agricultural insurance systems, and reduce economic losses from sudden climate-related disasters.
- Label: "Agricultural climate adaptation"
- Original ref: Measure 8.3.2
- Quality: Good target — action-oriented, addresses both capacity building and financial instruments (insurance). Directly relevant to NAP targets on disaster risk reduction.

**V2050_4** — *Pasture management and desertification*
> Establish a clear legal framework for pasture use, improvement, and protection; create a unified pasture management system to reduce pasture degradation and desertification.
- Label: "Pasture management & anti-desertification"
- Original ref: Measure 8.3.3
- Quality: Strong target — institutional (legal framework + management system) with clear environmental outcome (reduce degradation/desertification). Directly comparable to LDN and NBSAP targets.
- M&E status: 90% implementation

**V2050_5** — *Livestock-ecology balance*
> Set upper limits on livestock numbers, types, and composition aligned with ecology and pasture carrying capacity; combat pasture pests and rodents; and increase pasture, fodder, and water supply to reduce agricultural risks.
- Label: "Livestock-ecology balance"
- Original ref: Measure 8.3.4
- Quality: Strong target — multi-dimensional (ecological limits + pest management + water/fodder supply). Specific enough for alignment analysis.
- M&E status: 90% implementation

**V2050_6** — *Green economy livestock*
> Develop green economy-oriented livestock husbandry that preserves traditional nomadic cultural heritage in rangelands while establishing intensified, technology-driven farming near urban areas, sustainably using resources.
- Label: "Green economy livestock"
- Original ref: Measure 8.3.5
- Quality: Interesting target — bridges cultural preservation with green economy transition. Creates potential tension with intensification targets — valuable for coherence analysis.
- M&E status: 70% implementation

**V2050_7** — *Smart agriculture and soil*
> Develop smart technology-based agriculture to increase competitiveness and value of Mongolian crops grown in fertile soils, and increase agricultural exports.
- Label: "Smart agriculture & soil fertility"
- Original ref: Measure 8.3.6
- Quality: Moderate — combines soil fertility preservation with export/economic goals. The tension between export intensification and soil conservation is analytically interesting.

**V2050_8** — *Irrigated agriculture expansion*
> Expand irrigated agricultural production capacity by investing in modern irrigation infrastructure and bringing fallow lands into efficient cultivation.
- Label: "Irrigated agriculture expansion"
- Original ref: Measure 6.2.11
- Quality: Moderate — quantifiable (irrigation capacity in hectares), but primarily economic. Nature-climate relevance is through water resource management and sustainable land use.
- M&E status: 70% implementation

**V2050_9** — *Eco-initiatives in agriculture*
> Promote cooperative initiatives, eco-initiatives, and investment in agriculture to increase sustainable resource utilization.
- Label: "Agricultural eco-initiatives"
- Original ref: Measure 8.3.13
- Quality: Weak as standalone — vague and aspirational. Consider merging with V2050_6 or dropping.

**V2050_14** — *Ecosystem services valuation*

## Key observations for pipeline learning

1. **Vision 2050 targets lack time-binding**: Unlike NBSAP ("By 2030...") or NDC targets, Vision 2050 measures are structured as long-term aspirations (2020-2050) without intermediate deadlines. The pipeline extraction prompt expects time-bound details — this is a real pattern in national development plans vs. international commitments.

2. **Goal-level vs. measure-level**: Vision 2050 has a clear hierarchy (Goal → Objective → Measure). Our pipeline should extract at the **measure level** (8.3.3, 8.3.4) not the goal level (8.3). The goal-level V2050_2 is useful context but poor as a standalone target.

3. **Mongolian-language documents**: The extraction pipeline will need to handle Mongolian text. The M&E document has no English translation. The full LTDP 2050 has an official English translation available at cabinet.gov.mn but it's a simplified summary.

4. **Cross-ministry scope**: A single M&E report covers only one ministry's perspective. For comprehensive coherence analysis, we'd need Green Development targets from the Environment ministry's M&E report too. This is a data collection issue, not a pipeline issue.

5. **Pasture/livestock dominance**: Mongolia's agriculture sector is heavily pastoral — 7 of 9 nature-climate relevant targets relate to pasture/livestock. This concentration is genuine (not an extraction bias) and will produce interesting alignment patterns with NBSAP biodiversity targets.

6. **Pipeline improvement: extract activities when available**: Current pipeline extracts targets only. Should add a Phase 1.5 after target extraction: for each target, go back to source text and extract any listed sub-measures/activities. Strict rule: extract only what's explicitly there, return empty if none exist. This is NOT overfitting — it's making factual document content available to the alignment agents. Evidence: NBSAP/NAP targets with activities produce richer, more accurate alignment assessments than bare NDC targets without them.

7. **Pipeline improvement: track source page numbers per target**: The current extraction pipeline processes documents in chunks but does not record which page(s) each extracted target came from. This makes review and verification by policy experts difficult — they need to manually search the PDF. Fix: during chunk-based extraction, each chunk already knows its page range (from the PDF splitter). Propagate that metadata through to the extracted target as a `sourcePage` field (e.g., `"sourcePage": "33"` or `"sourcePage": "33-34"` for targets spanning pages). Low cost, high value for transparency and expert review workflows.

## Implementation steps

### Step 1: Add targets to data
1. **User reviews targets above** — provides feedback on wording, inclusions/exclusions
2. **Append to `python/data/mongolia-targets.json`** — add the 15 SECTORAL entries
3. **Regenerate TypeScript data** — `cd python && uv run python -m src.convert_to_ts`

### Step 2: Make all document types toggleable in dashboard
Currently BTR measures are toggleable. Extend this so ALL document types (NDC, NBSAP, NAP, SECTORAL, BTR) can be toggled on/off, letting users choose which policies to compare.

**Key files to explore:**
- `src/components/dashboard/dashboard-client.tsx` — main dashboard, likely where BTR toggle lives
- `src/components/viz/policy-coherence-explorer.tsx` — chord diagram, needs to filter by active docs
- `src/components/viz/alignment-network.tsx` — network viz, same filtering
- `src/lib/utils.ts` — utility functions for filtering/counting

**Approach:**
- Add a document filter UI (chip toggles or checkboxes) showing all available `sourceDocument` types
- Default: all enabled
- Filter targets, alignments, and classifications by selected documents
- All visualizations (chord, network, bar charts, contradiction summary) respect the filter
- Persist selection in URL params or local state so it survives tab switches

### Step 3: Verify
4. **Verify dashboard** — `pnpm dev` and check that SECTORAL targets appear correctly
5. **Toggle works** — disabling a document removes its targets and alignment pairs from all views
6. **Optionally re-run pipeline** — full analysis with new targets (~15 × 62 = 930 new alignment pairs)
