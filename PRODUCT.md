# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

UNDP country-office staff and national policy makers, most of them non-technical. They arrive with a live question about a country's nature-climate policy landscape (Mongolia, Panama, Sri Lanka, Côte d'Ivoire today, more to follow) and need an answer they can act on and defend, not a system to learn. Their job is to see whether a country's instruments (NDC, NBSAP, NAP, national development plans, and sectoral policies across agriculture, water, forest, and land use) actually reinforce each other toward major nature-climate targets, where money follows ambition, and whether commitments are being implemented. The outputs feed real decisions affecting people, ecosystems, and climate outcomes, and are validated with government sounding boards before wider distribution, so credibility and neutrality matter as much as insight.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered decision-support tool that helps assess coherence across nature-climate policies and track implementation progress. It works across three analytical levels: policy coherence (pairwise alignment surfacing synergies, potential misalignments, and gaps), financial and budget alignment (where money does and does not follow ambition), and implementation progress (whether policies are actually being carried out). Success is a policymaker walking away with an actionable, defensible read on their policy landscape that they could not have assembled from a quick internet search, grasped without technical training.

## Positioning

A decision-support system, not a decision-maker. It shows how a country's nature-climate policies do and do not reinforce each other as reviewable evidence, and hands final interpretation to the policymaker. Every surface prompts a closer look; none delivers a verdict.

## Brand Personality

Calm institutional authority is the base register: restrained, credible, safe to put in front of a government audience. On top of that it is deliberately accessible and guiding. Where a static tool would leave the user to dig, this one leads with the insight, tells a short dynamic story to get there, and hands over something actionable right away. Considered motion, clear visualization, and genuine UX craft are used so the analysis lands and, at moments, impresses, without ever tipping into decoration. Voice is third-person and institutional, never playful, no developer jargon, no em dashes; abbreviations are expanded or explained on first use.

## Anti-references

- AI-report slop: auto-generated consultancy dashboards, extended AI-written narrative reports, gradient hero-metric templates, generative filler presented as analysis.
- Alarmist or blame framing: red-alert risk dashboards, and any language that reads as assigning blame to a ministry, sector, or actor. The register is "opportunity for stronger alignment," never fault.
- Generic SaaS analytics: interchangeable startup-analytics look, identical card grids, tracked all-caps eyebrows above every section, charts as decoration.
- Academic density: jargon-heavy, information-dense output that a non-technical policymaker cannot parse at a glance.
- The generic "vibe-coded" application feel: templated, unconsidered, obviously machine-assembled. This should read as a purpose-built instrument, not a quick build.

## Design Principles

Real value-added or nothing. Every surface must measurably improve the decision-support a policymaker gets; demo polish, deadlines, and technical interestingness are not reasons to ship. If "would this improve the quality of decision-support?" is not a clear yes, cut it.

Decision-support, not decision-maker. Prefer visuals plus short factual insights over narrative; outputs are review prompts anchored to visible evidence, not findings, and final interpretation stays with the policymaker. AI outputs are labeled as such with confidence caveats.

Accessible over dense. Lead with the one insight a non-technical user can grasp in seconds and act on; let depth live behind a click, never on the face. Short dynamic storytelling and light hand-holding are welcome where they make the analysis land faster.

Neutral, never blame. Surface opportunities for stronger alignment in neutral language; never frame a result as fault toward a sector, ministry, or actor. Outputs are built to survive government sounding-board review.

Craft that earns trust. Motion, visualization, and interaction polish exist to make evidence legible and memorable, not to decorate. The interface should feel purpose-built and considered, so that the seriousness of the method is visible in the quality of the surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort, with no formal WCAG conformance target committed. Concrete accommodations already in place and to be preserved: `prefers-reduced-motion` is honored globally (animations collapse to static); color and contrast are worked against the UNDP palette; UI copy carries no developer jargon; abbreviations are expanded or given tooltips on first use. Where a design choice would trade legibility for elegance (light-gray body text, low-contrast tints), legibility wins.
